**Summary**

They revisit shuffle protocols for Shamir secret sharing and propose two new protocols that produce uniform shuffles. One is concretely efficient; the other achieves best-known O(n m l) online communication and O(n) rounds. The key is a permutation-sharing technique using smaller permutation matrices. They extend shuffle correlation to guarantee output delivery with linear online communication, yielding SLIDE—the first protocol to achieve both O(n m l) online communication and guaranteed output delivery.
