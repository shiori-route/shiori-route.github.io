**Summary**

Based on the title and abstract: LatencyLab is a DPDK-based framework to measure FPGA P4 pipeline latency on SmartNICs without PHC/PTP or clock sync. The switch multicasts probe packets so one copy traverses the P4 pipeline and another a bypass; a DPDK receiver busy-polls both ports and timestamps packets with the CPU TSC, and the arrival-time difference isolates pipeline latency after calibration with a null bitstream. They report tight, reproducible latency distributions on an AMD Alveo U280 and validate results with independent checks.

**Why it matters**

This is highly relevant to network-aware runtimes and any work that offloads computation to SmartNICs (including potential MPC accelerations). A synchronization-free, high-precision method to isolate on-NIC pipeline latency can inform protocol selection, performance modeling, and scheduling/round-coalescing decisions for distributed secure computations or low-latency RPC systems.

**Relation to current work**

If you study MPC offload to SmartNICs, network-aware runtimes, or need precise device-level latency numbers to optimize round coalescing and communication-computation overlap, LatencyLab's measurement technique and experimental results are directly applicable as a tool and methodology for evaluation.

**Transferable ideas**

Directly transferable: use the multicast-bypass + TSC-differencing technique to measure other SmartNIC pipelines or P4 implementations. Can feed precise latency data into performance models to guide network-aware scheduling, batching strategies, or decisions about which protocol steps to offload onto the NIC in high-latency deployments.
